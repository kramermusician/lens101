Cavalry, a beginner playlist.

Guide · Playlist · Mac and Windows · Free version

Cavalry is a free 2D motion design app. You build an animation out of shapes, duplicators, and behaviours, and the motion comes from the rules you set more than from drawing every frame. Start with video one. The player moves on to the next video by itself, or tap any video below to jump to it.

Getting started

  • Download Cavalry from cavalry.studio. The free version covers everything on this list.
  • Pause the video and copy each step as you go. The fastest way to learn is to make it yourself.
