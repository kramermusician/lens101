Blender, a beginner playlist.

Guide · Playlist · Mac and Windows · Free

Blender is free 3D software for modeling, animation, and rendering. It looks like a lot the first time you open it. The first video only covers moving around and the basic controls. That is enough to start.

Getting started

  • Download Blender from blender.org. It is free.
  • A mouse with a scroll wheel makes Blender much easier than a trackpad.
