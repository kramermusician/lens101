QR Scene, a city that turns into a code.

Three.js · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open QR Scene ›

Works in any browser on a laptop. Keep your phone camera ready.

What it is

From the side, it looks like a city of towers. Press Space and the camera flies up and looks straight down. The tops of the towers line up, and the city becomes a QR code. Point your phone at the screen and it opens a page.

Start here

  • Drag to look around the city.
  • Press Space to fly up. Press Space again to come back down.
  • Press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to change the link.
  • Press T to change the look: City, Equalizer or Wave.
  • Press H to hide the help and F for full screen.

Why it works

A QR code is only dark squares and light squares. Here every dark square is the top of a tower, and the ground is the light part. From the side, the heights hide the pattern. From straight above, you see only the tops, so the pattern comes back.

Tools used

  • Three.js for the 3D scene
  • Nothing is uploaded, and it needs no camera.
