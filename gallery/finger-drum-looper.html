Finger Drum Looper, a groovebox on your keyboard.

Web Audio + VexFlow · Laptop with a keyboard · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Finger Drum Looper ›

Works best on a laptop. Click the page once so it hears your keys.

What it is

Sixteen drum pads, each one a key on your keyboard. Hit record and play a beat with your fingers. The loop keeps going, so you can add a second take on top, then a third. As you play, the beat is written out as drum notation, so you can see what your hands just did.

Start here

  • Click the page once. Then try the keys printed on the pads.
  • Space plays and stops. Enter records. Backspace undoes your last take.
  • Hold Shift for a louder hit. G turns the click on, T taps the tempo, the arrows change tempo and kit.
  • Quantize snaps your hits to the beat: 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/8 triplets or 1/16 triplets. Set strength to 75% or 50% to keep some of your own feel. Snap all tightens hits you already recorded.
  • Map keys lets you move any sound to a different key.

Tools used

  • Web Audio for every sound. There are no samples; it's all synthesized in your browser.
  • VexFlow draws the notation.
  • No account, no install, nothing saved online.
