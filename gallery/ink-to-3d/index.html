3. Export

GLB or OBJ? Both are 3D models of the same shape. GLB is one small file that also carries your name and the color you picked, so it lands in Blender ready to go. OBJ is an older, plain-text format with only the shape, no color. Almost every 3D app can open an OBJ, so use it when an app will not take a GLB. For Blender, pick GLB.
In Blender: File, Import, glTF 2.0 (.glb). The letters stand up facing the front view, centered on the origin, ready to animate. For softer edges add a Bevel modifier.
