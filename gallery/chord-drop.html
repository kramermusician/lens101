Chord Drop, a chord chart with the rhythm in it.

SVG + Web Audio · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Chord Drop ›

Works in any browser on a laptop. On a phone or iPad, the big pad stands in for the space bar.

What it is

A chord sheet that says G C D G tells the band which chords to play but not when. Chord Drop fills that in. You type the chords, play along with a drum groove, and press the space bar each time the next chord comes in. It snaps your taps to the beat and prints a chart with every chord placed where it lands, over a line of rhythm notation that shows how long it rings.

Start here

  • Type the chords under section names in square brackets, like [Verse] and [Chorus]. Write a name again with nothing under it to repeat that section.
  • Pick a tempo and a beat: Click, Rock, Shuffle or Funk.
  • Pick a section and press Record. After the count-in, the first chord starts on beat 1. Press space for each next chord, then once more where the section ends.
  • Press Play whole song to hear it back. Click any chord on the chart to move it a little earlier or later.
  • Save PDF prints the chart. Choose Save as PDF in the print window.

Tools used

  • SVG for the notation, Web Audio for the drums and chords
  • No build step, nothing to install, and nothing is uploaded. Songs stay in your browser, in a song file you save, or in a link you send.
