Key Party, a music theory party game.

Canvas + Web Audio + Gamepad · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Key Party ›

Best in Chrome on a big screen with two to four gamepads. Two players can share one keyboard.

What it is

Four fast minigames that test how quickly you know your keys. Every round is a race, and you can punch your friends to make them drop what they are carrying.

  • Bucket Brigade: the banner shows a key, like A major. Grab its sharps or flats and drop them in your bucket before time runs out.
  • Scale Stampede: step on the notes of the scale in order, root to root. Major and Mixolydian.
  • Chord Corners: the banner shows a chord. Run to the corner with the right spelling.
  • 12 Bar Blues Lava: a blues plays and every player gets their own piano. Stand on a note of the chord that is playing right now. Every other key is lava.

Start here

  • Plug in your gamepads and press a button on each one so the browser sees it.
  • Press X to join. Options starts the match.
  • No gamepads? Player 1 uses WASD with F, G and H. Player 2 uses the arrow keys with comma, period and slash.
  • On a gamepad, X grabs and drops, square punches, and triangle uses a power-up.

Tools used

  • HTML canvas for the arena, Web Audio for the blues band, the Gamepad API for controllers
  • No build step, nothing to install, and nothing is uploaded. Scores stay in your browser.
