1Type your chords

How to type a song
  • Put each section name in square brackets, like [Verse] or [Chorus].
  • Under it, type the chords in the order they come, with spaces: G C D G. No timing needed. You tap that in next.
  • Want a section again? Write its name again with nothing under it. The chart reuses it.
  • x2 after a line plays that line twice. [Chorus x2] plays the whole section twice.
  • N.C. means no chord, the band stops.
  • Just chords here. The words go in step 4, after you tap.
  • Pasting a chord sheet that has words in it? That's fine. A button shows up to move the words into step 4.
  • Already know the bars? Use | between bars and / for extra beats: | G / C / | D |. That section comes out ready, no tapping.

3Tap the chord changes

Pick a section and press Record. After the count-in, the first chord starts on beat 1 by itself. Press Space each time the next chord comes in. After the last chord, press Space once more where the section ends.

4Add the words

Under each chord, type what you sing while it plays. Enter goes to the next chord. To split a word across two chords, end the first box with a dash: mor- then ning.

Fix tap timing

Speakers and Bluetooth headphones add a delay, so taps can land late. Press Start, listen to four clicks, then tap Space (or the pad) right on each of the next twelve clicks.
