Zooparty, a zoetrope you draw on a spinning record.

Canvas · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Zooparty ›

Best on an iPad with an Apple Pencil. Works on a phone or a laptop too.

What it is

A zoetrope is a ring of drawings that turns into a moving picture when it spins. Put one on a record player, film it, and every frame of the video catches the next drawing in the ring. That is the trick behind the spinning-disc animations in Jacob Collier's videos, and it is the whole idea here. The canvas is a record. Dotted lines cut it into slices, and each slice is one frame. Draw in the lit slice at the top, turn the record one frame, and draw the next step. Press play to spin it, then turn on the strobe and watch it move.

Start here

  • Press play, then the lightning button. The starter record has a ball that bounces in and out, a plus that twirls, and a dot that orbits.
  • Draw something small in the lit slice at the top. Press the arrow to turn one frame and draw it again, moved a little. Turn on the eye button to see the frames on either side as red and green ghosts.
  • Turn on Echo and draw once. Your mark lands in every frame. Add Out, Twirl, or Grow and each copy moves a little more than the one before.
  • Pinch to zoom in for details. Twist two fingers to turn the record, or grab the rim and spin it by hand. With an Apple Pencil, the pencil draws and your fingers move the record. Tap with two fingers to undo.
  • Add a photo to place it in the top slice. Pick a handful of photos and they deal out one per frame, so a burst of shots becomes a loop.
  • The Disc button changes the record color and the label. The chip at the top sets the speed.

Make a real one

  • The menu prints a template at 7, 10, or 12 inches with a cut line and the spindle hole marked. Glue it to card and put it on a turntable.
  • Film it with your phone. The Frames panel tells you how many frames match your camera. At 33⅓ RPM and 30 frames a second, that is 54.
  • Use bright light so the camera picks a fast shutter, or the frames will smear.

Your files

Nothing you make is uploaded. The page saves itself in your browser, Save gives you a project file (name.zoo.json), and the menu exports a picture, a spinning GIF, a one-slice animation GIF, or a short video.

Tools used

  • HTML canvas and pointer events, with pencil pressure and pencil-only drawing
  • MediaRecorder for video; one small library (gif.js), fetched only when you export a GIF
  • One file, no build step
