LoopGrid, a video looper in your browser.

Camera + microphone · Browser · Laptop · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open LoopGrid ›

Made for a laptop with a keyboard. Chrome or Edge runs it best; Firefox and Safari work too.

Get the Mac app

Want the full desktop version? Download LoopGrid for Mac. It works on any Mac, Apple silicon or Intel.

Download LoopGrid 0.4 for Mac ›

1.8 MB zip

  • Open the zip, then drag LoopGrid into your Applications folder.
  • Open LoopGrid. macOS will say it can't check the app, because it isn't from the App Store. That is expected.
  • Click Done, then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to LoopGrid. Enter your password.
  • Allow the camera and the microphone when it asks. You only do this once.

What it is

Press the spacebar and LoopGrid records a video of you with its sound. Press it again and that take starts looping right away. Record another and it locks in time with the first. Each loop gets its own tile, and the grid grows as you add more, up to nine. It works like a looper pedal, except every layer is also a video of you playing it.

Start here

  • Allow the camera and the microphone when the browser asks.
  • Wear headphones. Then the loops don't feed back into the microphone.
  • Press Space to record. Clap, sing, or tap a beat. Press Space again to close the loop.
  • Press Space again to record the next layer over the top. It snaps to the length of the first loop.
  • Press T a few times to tap a tempo before the first loop, then C for a count-in and M for a click.
  • Press I for instant loop mode: one press closes a take and starts the next one.
  • Press R to record the whole performance. The video downloads when you press R again.
  • The ? key shows every key, and the comma key opens Settings.

Lining up picture and sound

Webcams send the picture a little after the sound. Record a loop of yourself clapping, then press the [ and ] keys until each clap looks and sounds like one moment.

Your files

Nothing leaves your computer. There is no account and no server; your loops live in the browser's memory while the tab is open. Keep the tab in front while you play, since a hidden tab stops drawing. If long loops slow things down, pick a smaller frame size in Settings.

Tools used

  • JavaScript and HTML, with no libraries
  • An AudioWorklet for sound, with every loop timed by the audio clock
  • Canvas for the grid, and MediaRecorder for the performance video
