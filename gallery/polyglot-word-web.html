Polyglot Word Web, seven languages you can wander through.

Canvas + plain JavaScript · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Polyglot Word Web ›

Best on a laptop. Each language loads the first time you pick it.

What it is

A map of words, drawn as dots and lines. Pick French, Spanish, Korean, Chinese, Japanese, Serbian or Greek, type a word, and it opens with its meaning, an example sentence, all its forms, and the words it connects to. Click a neighbor and keep going. Every word also shows the same word in the other languages, so you can jump from manger to 먹다 to 吃 in three clicks.

Start here

  • Type any form you've seen: fuimos finds ser, 먹었어요 finds 먹다, 食べました finds 食べる.
  • English works too. Type house or to run in any language.
  • Korean, Chinese, Japanese and Greek also take Latin letters: kalimera, nihao, gakkou.
  • Click a dot on the map to open it. Drag to move, scroll to zoom, and switch to Whole web to see every word at once.
  • Random drops you somewhere new. The arrow takes you back.
  • About explains where the words, forms and sentences came from.

A note on the sentences

The forms are generated and checked against outside sources. The meanings, links and example sentences were written with AI and haven't been read by a native speaker yet, so if a sentence sounds off, it might be. New words get added over time.

Tools used

  • Canvas for the map and plain JavaScript for everything else, with no libraries
  • Word data from open sources (Wiktionary, CC-CEDICT, JMdict, OpenSubtitles frequency lists), credited in About
  • Nothing you type leaves your browser.
