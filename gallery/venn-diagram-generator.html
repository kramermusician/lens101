Venn Diagram Generator, vocabulary in every overlap.

SVG + Claude API · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open the generator ›

Works in any browser. Tap a section to see every word in it.

What it is

Pick how many circles you want, name them or just give a topic, and Claude builds a Venn diagram full of English words. Every section gets words at three levels: A1 for everyday words, B1 for useful middle words, and C1 for precise, advanced ones. A word in the middle has to fit every circle around it, so the overlaps are where the good arguments start.

Start here

  • Choose 2, 3 or 4 circles. Type a topic, circle names, or both.
  • Press Make the diagram. It takes about half a minute.
  • Tap any section to see all its words, each with a meaning and an example sentence.
  • Turn A1, B1 or C1 on and off to fit the class.
  • Copy share link sends the finished diagram to anyone. Opening a link needs no key and costs nothing.

Tools used

  • SVG for the diagram, Claude Haiku 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5 for the words
  • Making a new diagram needs an Anthropic API key, saved only in your own browser. A Haiku diagram costs well under a cent.
