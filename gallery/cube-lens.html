Cube Lens, a Rubik's cube in your hands.

MediaPipe + Three.js · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open the Virtual Cube ›

No cube needed. Allow the camera. Use Chrome on a laptop.

Open Cube Lens ›

Hold a real Rubik's cube up to the camera.

What it is

The camera finds your hands. In the Virtual Cube, a 3D Rubik's cube floats between them, and you can spin it and turn its layers in the air. In Cube Lens, you hold a real cube, and the camera finds it between your hands and puts effects on it.

Virtual Cube

  • Hold up both hands, open. The cube floats between them and grows when you move your hands apart.
  • Tilt your hands to roll the cube. Move one hand closer to the camera to turn it.
  • Make a fist and move it to spin the whole cube.
  • Pinch a sticker and swipe to turn that layer.
  • Keys: U D L R F B turn a layer (Shift turns it back), S scrambles, 0 resets. The mouse works too.

Cube Lens

  • Hold the cube with both hands, sticker side to the camera.
  • Press 1 to 9 or Q to U to change the effect, or Space to cycle.
  • Press D to see what the camera is tracking.

Tools used

  • MediaPipe for the hands, Three.js for the 3D cube
  • The video stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.
