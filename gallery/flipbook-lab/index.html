Flipbook Lab

A small animation studio that runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your computer: your work autosaves in this browser, and Save downloads a project file you can open again anywhere.

Three ways to animate
  • Frame by frame: draw, press N for a new frame, draw the next pose with the onion skin showing the last one.
  • Stop motion: press Camera, build something on your desk, capture a frame, move it a little, capture again.
  • Keyframes: draw something on a layer, press "Make motion layer", go to a later frame, drag the object. The frames in between are filled in for you. Change the ease to change the feel.
Habits that make animation better
  • Save a Version before a big change. Versions are cheap. Try the risky idea, then compare.
  • Duplicate a frame and nudge it instead of redrawing from nothing.
  • Hold frames: two frames held for two is often smoother than four drawings. Press + on a frame.
  • Squash and stretch: on a motion layer set Scale X up and Scale Y down at the moment of impact.
  • Ease in and ease out: things in the world speed up and slow down. Linear looks like a robot. Sometimes that is the point.
  • Watch it loop twenty times. The mistake you see on loop twelve is the note.

Files

Save writes name.flipbook.json. Open reads it back. Export makes a GIF, a WebM video, a PNG of this frame, a sprite sheet, or every frame as PNGs. Import lets you drop images in as frames or as a background.
