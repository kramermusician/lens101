Paper Flipbook, a whole animation on one sheet of paper.

Canvas · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Paper Flipbook ›

Opens in a new tab. Works best on your phone, since that is where the camera is.

What it is

You draw every frame of an animation in the squares of one printed sheet, take one photo of the page, and the tool cuts it apart and plays it back. You get a GIF, a video, every frame as images, or a sprite sheet. Pencil, marker, collage, anything that fits in a square counts.

Start here

  • Pick a grid and print the sheet. Start with 3 by 3. Nine frames is plenty for a first loop.
  • Draw left to right, top to bottom, and change each frame a little. Keep the drawing inside the squares.
  • Lay the page flat in even light and take one photo with all four black corner marks in the shot. An angle is fine. Blur is not.
  • Check that the red dots sit on the corner marks, then play it. Tap any frame to skip it, change the speed, try back and forth.
  • Export a GIF and you are done.

No printer?

Rule your own grid of equal squares by hand, choose My own grid in step 3, and drag the red dots onto its four outer corners.

Your files

Your photo stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
