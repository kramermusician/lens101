Score Sketch, sheet music at typing speed.

SVG + Web Audio · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Score Sketch ›

Built for a laptop keyboard. The note buttons on the toolbar work on an iPad too.

What it is

A music notebook that writes as fast as you can type. Press a letter from A to G and that note lands on the staff, in the octave closest to the note before it. Bars fill up on their own, and a note too long for the bar gets split and tied across the barline for you. Press space to hear it.

Start here

  • Click the title and the composer to type your own.
  • Type letters A to G for notes and R for a rest. Shift with the up or down arrow moves a note up or down an octave.
  • The [ and ] keys make the selected note shorter or longer, and the period key dots it.
  • Press V to switch to voice 2 and write a second line on the same staff, stems down.
  • Click a note to select it, then use the toolbar for dynamics, crescendos, repeat signs, first and second endings, and time or key changes from that bar on.
  • Add a text box for performance notes. Drag it by its top bar and resize it from the corner.
  • Print / PDF saves a clean page. MusicXML opens in MuseScore or Noteflight. Copy link sends the whole score in one link.

Tools used

  • SVG for the notation, drawn with the free Bravura music font, and Web Audio for playback
  • Your score saves itself in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.
