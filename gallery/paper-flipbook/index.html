Paper Flipbook

Draw a whole animation on one sheet of paper, snap a photo, and get back a GIF, a video, or frames.

1

Print the sheet

Every sheet fits US Letter (8.5 × 11 in). The four black corner marks let the app find the grid even when the photo is tilted or taken at an angle. The QR code on the sheet opens this app with that grid already chosen. Print at 100% or fit to page; either works.

Long-press the template image to save it, then print it.

Draw frames left to right, top to bottom. Keep drawings inside the squares. Pick the same grid size here and in step 3 as the sheet you printed.

2

Photograph it

  • Lay the sheet flat and fit all four corner marks in the shot.
  • Even light helps most. Avoid your own shadow across the page.
  • An angle is fine. Blur is not.

3

Line up the corners

Choose a photo first.

Each red dot belongs on the center of a black corner mark. Drag to adjust; a magnifier appears while you drag.

If frame 1 isn't in the top-left of your drawing, tap Rotate until it is.

4

Preview and tune

Tap a frame to skip or restore it.
5

Export

Photos stay on your device; nothing is uploaded.
