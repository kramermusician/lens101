The Belafonte, a living cross-section.

Three.js · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the canvas to look around the ship.
  • Use the zoom slider below the canvas to push in and out.
  • Press P to fly the Sea Hawk helicopter. Arrow keys steer.
  • Press B to dive the yellow submarine. Arrow keys steer.

Why it is here

This piece is the kind of thing the class is about. It started as a curiosity about how Wes Anderson's cross-section poster for The Life Aquatic actually works as a moving system, and it ended up as a small browser toy you can pilot. The tool was Three.js, but the question came first.

When you start your own work this fall, that is the loop I want you in. Find an image, an object, a moment in a film, a piece of music, anything that snags you. Pick a tool that lets you take it apart. Make a small thing. Show it. Then make a slightly less small thing.

Tools used

  • Three.js (WebGL renderer, scene graph, materials)
  • Hand-authored geometry (no imported models)
  • Plain HTML and CSS for the HUD overlay
  • No build step, no bundler, runs from a folder of files
