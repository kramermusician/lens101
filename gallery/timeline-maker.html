What it is

A timeline you can shape. Drop photos onto the page, type a title and a date for each one, and the events put themselves in order. Drag an event along the line and its date follows. Then bend the line into a wave, a spiral, a rainbow, or a board game path that winds across the whole screen.

Start here

  • Open the History of the Blues and press Play. The events appear one at a time while the line draws itself.
  • Press New, then drop a few photos from your own life onto the Events panel. Give each one a year.
  • If you do not know a date, choose No date and the event sits between its neighbors.
  • Turn on a second timeline in a different color. Put your life next to an artist's life and compare them.
  • Try the Style and Text tabs: shapes, rulers, card styles, fonts, and colors. Candyland lets you pick the colors of the board.
  • Press Orbit and the timeline becomes a ring. Press Flat to go back to the regular line at the same event.

Your files

Nothing you make is uploaded. The page autosaves in your browser, but only on this computer, and lots of photos can fill it up. Save file gives you one small project file with your photos inside it, named name.timeline.json. Open it later on any computer. Export PNG gives you a picture of the whole timeline.

The blues starter

Every photo in the starter is public domain or openly licensed, and the credits print at the bottom of the timeline. The full list is in CREDITS.md.

Tools used

  • SVG for the timeline, drawn fresh every time you change something
  • Three.js for Orbit, fetched only when you press it
  • One file, no build step
