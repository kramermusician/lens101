Speech Silhouette, your body written in your own words.

MediaPipe + Canvas + Web Speech · Browser · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Speech Silhouette ›

When it asks, allow the camera and the microphone. Use Chrome on a laptop with a webcam.

What it is

The camera finds your body and cuts it out of the room. Then it fills that shape with the last thing you said. Talk, and the words inside you change. Raise both hands and the sentence hangs like a string between your fingertips.

Start here

  • Pick a language, press Start, and allow the camera and the microphone.
  • Step back so the camera sees your body, then say something.
  • Press one letter to change the language, like J for Japanese or K for Korean.
  • Press the up and down arrows to change the text size. Tap twice fast for a big jump.
  • Press the left and right arrows to change the look. Camera color paints each letter with the color the camera sees.
  • Press R to record a video, R again to stop, and Shift+P to save a picture.
  • Press G, type a sentence, and say it until every word turns green.
  • Press the comma key to open the settings. Choose your microphone and camera there.

Tools used

  • MediaPipe for the body outline and the hands, Canvas for the text
  • Chrome turns your speech into text. Chrome sends that sound to Google to do it, so it needs the internet.
  • Videos and pictures save to your own computer, and nothing else is uploaded.
