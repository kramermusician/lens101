Jam Box, a beat you build with friends.

Web Audio + Firebase · Phone or laptop · 2026 · Kramer Gibson

Open Jam Box ›

One to four players, each on their own phone or laptop. Bring headphones.

What it is

A loop maker for up to four people at once. One of you starts a jam and gets a four-letter code. Everyone else types the code and you're all in the same song. There are drums, four bars of chords, and two melody voices. Anyone can change anything, and every change lands on every phone. If your friend speeds up the tempo, it speeds up for you too.

Start here

  • Put your headphones on. Everyone hears the same loop at the same time on their own phone.
  • Tap Start a jam, then send your friends the code. Tap the code to share a link.
  • Press play. Tap squares in Drums to add hits. Pick a chord for each bar in Chords.
  • In Voice 1 and Voice 2, tap the piano roll, or switch to Keyboard and play. Every note on the keyboard fits the key, so nothing sounds wrong.
  • Press the red button to record. Play the pads or keys and your notes stick in the loop. On a laptop, the A S D F G H J K L ; keys play too.
  • Your squares are your color, so you can see who added what.
  • When you like it, the three dots have Save audio. It records the loop twice and downloads it.

Tools used

  • Web Audio for every sound. There are no samples; it's all synthesized in your browser.
  • Firebase keeps the room in sync. Nothing is saved but the loop itself and the name you type.
  • No account, no install. "Just me" mode works with no internet.
